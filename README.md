# Pomodoro Web Timer ⏳🍅

## Overview

A productivity tool designed to help you manage your time efficiently. With great performance, it divides your work into 25-minute intervals, known as Pomodoro 🍅, followed by 5-minute short breaks ⏳. Additionally, after completing four Pomodoro intervals, you take a longer break of 15 minutes ☕. This methodology allows you to maintain high levels of concentration and avoid burnout, thanks to its beautiful and efficient design 🌟.
